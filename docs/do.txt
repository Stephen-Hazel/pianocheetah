   look at the pc's barcode sticker and google the model of pc with bios key
      bios menus work best with wired keyboards and mice

      now you answer the installer questions then (time it)
      it'll say "time to reboot", hit ok
      it'll remind you to take out the usb stick
   now you configure kde
