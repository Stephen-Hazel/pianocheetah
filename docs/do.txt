shorty is my van piano.

music is a big thing and I'm not talking about all of it.
   I'm here to play the music.  Not compose or mixdown, etc
   To learn the notes, play them in time, adjust the tempo as i play,
   and hit the volumes of eeeach note at to make the music swell and ebb.
      To give it feel.
   I'm talking about playing for an audience
      so no slow parts or fumbling or places where I'm not in control.
      The beat must be kept and the listeners won't need to give me that
         "oh, you did pretty good" look.
   I'm expecting the same smile I felt when picking the song.
   I'm here to make the piano and drums do what my voice does - really sing.

   But I'm also not here to become a proper piano player.
      I don't care about reading sheet music or playing scales.
      I don't wanna play the whole song by memory - hitting every key perfectly.
      I wanna play a song without grinding my teeth.
         And maybe even sing along as I play.
      You know who's good at hitting every note perfectly?  A computer.
         But they're no good at swells and ebbs and feel.
         Computers like playing every note at exactly the same volume.
         duuumb.

      So I want to play in a less demanding way.
         But with all of the feels.
         That feel can be different from one day to the next.
         My feelings change.  There's more than one way to play a song.
